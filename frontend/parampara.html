Local Culinary Treasures

Hyderabadi Biryani

A fragrant rice dish cooked with marinated meat and a blend of spices, slow-cooked to perfection.

Origin: 18th Century

Gujarati Dhokla

A savory steamed cake made from fermented rice and chickpea batter, light and fluffy in texture.

Origin: Ancient Gujarat

Bengali Rasgulla

Soft, spongy cheese balls soaked in light sugar syrup, a signature sweet from West Bengal.

Origin: 19th Century

Masala Dosa

A crispy fermented crepe made from rice batter, stuffed with spiced potatoes, served with chutney.

Origin: South India
